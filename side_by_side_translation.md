# Turkish Conversation - Side-by-Side Translation & Grammar

## 1. hüsamettin: Of çok kötü olmuştur.

| Turkish | Literal | Grammar |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Of | Oh/Ugh | Interjection |
| çok | very | Adverb |
| kötü | bad | Adjective |
| olmuştur | has become | ol- (become) + -muş (evidential past) + -tur (formal 3rd person) |

**English:** "Oh, it has become very bad."
**Grammar Notes:** Evidential past tense (-muş) suggests speaker learned this information indirectly. Formal register with -tur ending.

## 2. Beyza: Evet iğrenç kokuyorlar.

| Turkish | Literal | Grammar |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Evet | Yes | Affirmative |
| iğrenç | disgusting | Adjective |
| kokuyorlar | they smell | kok- (smell) + -uyor (present continuous) + -lar (3rd plural) |

**English:** "Yes, they smell disgusting."
**Grammar Notes:** Present continuous tense. Subject "they" is incorporated in verb ending -lar.

## 7. Beyza: Eee konuşma biz benim alt katımda iranlılar oturuyordu.

| Turkish | Literal | Grammar |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Eee | well/umm | Hesitation particle |
| konuşma | speaking of | Verbal noun |
| biz | we | 1st person plural pronoun |
| benim | my | 1st person possessive pronoun |
| alt | lower | Adjective |
| katımda | on my floor | kat (floor) + -ım (1st possessive) + -da (locative) |
| iranlılar | Iranians | İran + -lı (from) + -lar (plural) |
| oturuyordu | were living | otur- (sit/live) + -uyor (continuous) + -du (past) |

**English:** "Well, speaking of that, Iranians were living on my lower floor."
**Grammar Notes:** Past continuous tense. Possessive + locative case: "on my floor."

## 10. Beyza: İlk eve taşındığımda biz onlardan sonra taşındık.

| Turkish | Literal | Grammar |
|---------|---------|---------|
| İlk | first | Adverb |
| eve | to house | ev (house) + -e (dative case) |
| taşındığımda | when I moved | taşın- (move) + -dığ (past participle) + -ım (1st person) + -da (temporal) |
| biz | we | 1st person plural |
| onlardan | from them | onlar (they) + -dan (ablative case) |
| sonra | after | Adverb |
| taşındık | we moved | taşın- (move) + -dı (past) + -k (1st plural) |

**English:** "When I first moved to the house, we moved after them."
**Grammar Notes:** Temporal clause with -dığımda "when I...". Ablative case -dan shows "from/after them."

## 11. Beyza: Odadan çıkamadım, inan o kadar korktum ki?

| Turkish | Literal | Grammar |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Odadan | from room | oda (room) + -dan (ablative) |
| çıkamadım | I couldn't exit | çık- (exit) + -ama (negative ability) + -dı (past) + -m (1st person) |
| inan | believe | Imperative form of inan- |
| o kadar | so much | Demonstrative + degree |
| korktum | I got scared | kork- (fear) + -tu (past) + -m (1st person) |
| ki | that | Result conjunction |

**English:** "I couldn't get out of the room, believe me, I was so scared!"
**Grammar Notes:** Negative ability -ama + past tense. "O kadar...ki" construction shows degree/result.

## 13. Beyza: Kavga oluyor zannettim. Meğer babamı anlattım falan böyle böyle bir şey ol.

| Turkish | Literal | Grammar |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Kavga | fight | Noun |
| oluyor | is happening | ol- (become) + -uyor (present continuous) |
| zannettim | I thought | zannet- (suppose) + -ti (past) + -m (1st person) |
| Meğer | turns out | Realization adverb |
| babamı | my father (obj) | baba + -m (possessive) + -ı (accusative) |
| anlattım | I told | anlat- (tell) + -tı (past) + -m (1st person) |
| falan | and such | Informal particle |
| böyle böyle | like this like that | Repetition for vagueness |
| bir şey | something | Indefinite |
| ol | be/happen | Incomplete verb |

**English:** "I thought a fight was happening. Turns out I told my father about it and such, something like that happened."
**Grammar Notes:** "Meğer" shows realization of misunderstanding. Sentence trails off incomplete (informal speech).

## Key Grammar Patterns:

1. **Case System**: Locative (-da), Ablative (-dan), Dative (-e), Accusative (-ı), Genitive (-ın)
2. **Verb Tenses**: Present continuous (-iyor), Past (-dı/-ti), Evidential past (-muş)
3. **Possessive**: Personal possessive suffixes attached to nouns
4. **Questions**: Particle mı/mi + appropriate copula
5. **Informal Speech**: Hesitation particles, incomplete sentences, repetition
