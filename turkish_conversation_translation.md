## Key Grammar Patterns Explained

### 1. Case System
- **Dative (-a/-e)**: "altına" (underneath), "bana" (to me), "haftaya" (for next week)
- **Ablative (-dan/-den)**: "altından" (from underneath)
- **Accusative (-ı/-i/-u/-ü)**: "seni" (you-object)

### 2. Verb Conjugations
- **Negative Past**: "bakmadık" (we didn't look)
- **Present Continuous Negative**: "gelmiyor" (isn't coming)
- **Future**: "olacak" (will be)
- **Passive**: "yenilendi" (was renewed)

### 3. Discourse Particles
- **"ya"**: Interjection meaning "well/oh"
- **"işte"**: "there you go/well" - conclusion marker
- **"yalnız"**: "though/however" - contrast marker
- **"falan"**: "and such/etc." - approximation/indefiniteness

### 4. Question Formation
- **"ne oldu?"**: What happened? (interrogative + past tense)
- **"naze/neden"**: Why? (interrogative adverb)

### 5. Existential Constructions
- **"var"**: There is/exists
- **"yok"**: There isn't/doesn't exist
- **"çok yok"**: There isn't much (quantity + negative existential)
